Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

One Thing is a daily reading app that gives you one research finding a day, in five minutes. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Email address — if you sign in with email (magic link) or Google. Used to identify your account.
  • Reading activity — which papers you read, quiz answers, highlights, and streaks. Used to personalise your daily paper and track your progress.
  • Preferences — your topic interests, difficulty setting, and reading duration. Used to choose the right paper for you.
  • Name — optional, if you add it in your profile. Used to personalise your greeting.

We do not collect your location, contacts, payment information, or device identifiers. We do not run ads.

How we use it

  • To select and serve your daily paper
  • To track your reading streak and XP
  • To remember your highlights and saved notes
  • To send you your magic link when you sign in

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase (database and auth) — stores your account and reading data on servers in Ireland (EU). GDPR-compliant. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google (if you use Google sign-in) — we receive only your name and email from Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Vercel (hosting) — serves the app. Sees standard web request data (IP, browser). vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days. We may delete inactive accounts (no sign-in for 2 years) with 30 days notice.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Delete — delete your account and all associated data from the app (Settings → You → Delete my account)
  • Correct — update your name, email, or preferences at any time in settings
  • Object — stop us processing your data (the practical outcome is account deletion)
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format

These rights apply under GDPR (EU) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Cookies and storage

We use localStorage in your browser to store your reading state and preferences locally. This is functional storage — it makes the app work. It is not used for tracking or advertising, and does not require consent under the ePrivacy Directive.

We do not use cookies for analytics or advertising.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access is controlled by Row Level Security — you can only read and write your own data. We use Supabase Auth for identity management, which does not store passwords (we use magic links and Google OAuth only).

Children

One Thing is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will notify you by email before making material changes to this policy. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version.

Contact

Questions, data requests, or concerns: privacy@read-one-thing.vercel.app
